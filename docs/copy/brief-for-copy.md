# octate studio: copy brief (source of truth for the copywriter)

## Who we are
octate studio is a small, AI-accelerated web studio. Always lowercase "octate studio".
Voice: "we" / "the studio". No founder name. Tagline: "Premium websites, live in 7 days."
Real facts we can use: close to a dozen projects shipped across web and apps, and over
1M impressions on social content we've made. Nothing else is proven. Do not invent
numbers, clients, results, testimonials or awards.

## Who reads this
(a) Owner-led local businesses in India and Nepal: clinics, dental and skin practices,
    real estate, coaching institutes, interior designers, premium cafes and hotels.
(b) Med spas in the US, UK and Australia.
They arrive from OUR cold call / WhatsApp / email, open the link on an Android phone,
and decide in about 10 seconds: "Is this studio real, good, and safe to pay?"

## Hard rules (break any and the copy is rejected)
- NEVER use em dashes (—) or en dashes used as em dashes. Use commas, periods, colons, parentheses.
- Banned words: elevate, unlock, unleash, seamless, cutting-edge, synergy, leverage,
  bespoke digital experiences, empower, revolutionize, game-changer, next-level, robust,
  in today's fast-paced world, look no further. Also avoid: transform, journey, delve,
  tailored solutions, digital presence, take it to the next level, supercharge.
- No fake scarcity, no countdowns, no fake strikethrough prices, no fake "trusted by".
- Any guarantee or risk-reversal you propose that is NOT already in the offer above must
  be marked [NEEDS OWNER APPROVAL] because the studio has to honour it.
- No emoji in headings. Plain, short sentences. Concrete nouns ("more booked
  consultations"), never abstractions ("enhanced digital presence").
- /med-spa forms collect contact details only, never health info.
