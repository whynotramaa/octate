# octate studio: copy deck v2

Source of truth: brief-for-copy.md. Every number below comes from the brief. Where a line needs a fact the brief does not give, it is marked [CONFIRM] and a safe fallback is supplied.

## Voice guide

1. Talk like a founder across a table: "we", plain nouns, short sentences. Do: "You send us a brief. We build the site." Don't: "We deliver holistic web solutions."
2. Name the outcome, not the activity. Do: "More booked consultations." Don't: "Enhanced online visibility."
3. Shrink the effort, raise the certainty. Do: "One call from you. We do the rest." Don't: "Our streamlined process ensures success."
4. Respect the owner's time and the work they already do. Do: "You run a clinic. You do not have time to chase a web designer." Don't: "Struggling with your online presence?"
5. Stay calm and premium. State facts, never beg. Do: "Fixed price. Pay half after you approve." Don't: "Hurry, spots are going fast!"
6. Zero hype, zero dashes, zero fake proof. Do: "Close to a dozen projects shipped." Don't: "Trusted by hundreds of brands."

## Proposed guarantees and risk reversals [NEEDS OWNER APPROVAL]

None of these are in the current offer. Do not publish any of them until the owner signs off.

1. **Deposit refund before the build starts** [NEEDS OWNER APPROVAL]
   Copy: "Change your mind before Day 2? Get your deposit back."
   Cost if invoked: the full 50% deposit goes back. On Launch that is roughly ₹7,500 (India), रू12,500 (Nepal) or $300 (international) at founding prices, plus the time spent on the call and brief.

2. **Late launch credit** [NEEDS OWNER APPROVAL]
   Copy: "If we miss your launch date, you get a credit on your next invoice."
   Cost if invoked: whatever credit the owner chooses. Needs a defined amount and a rule for delays caused by the client (late approvals, late content).

3. **One free design reset** [NEEDS OWNER APPROVAL]
   Copy: "Not feeling the first direction? We start the design again once."
   Cost if invoked: roughly the Day 2 to 6 build time again, which is the most expensive part of the project, and it would sit on top of the included revision rounds.

4. **Free preview before payment** [NEEDS OWNER APPROVAL]
   Copy: "See your site before you pay anything."
   Cost if invoked: a full build with no deposit. If the prospect does not proceed, the studio loses that build time. Note that /for/[slug] demos may already do this, which should be confirmed.

5. **Audit at no charge** [NEEDS OWNER APPROVAL]
   Copy: "Your audit costs nothing."
   Cost if invoked: studio time for each audit, including audits from people who never buy.
